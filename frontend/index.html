HOW EVASION DETECTION WORKS
METHOD 1
Z-Score Variance Analysis
Legitimate traffic shows natural variance. Suspiciously LOW variance in Z-scores = attacker is rate-controlling to stay under threshold.
METHOD 2
Threshold Proximity Clustering
Flags IPs whose Z-scores cluster in the 1.8–2.45 zone — just below the 2.5 detection threshold. Random traffic doesn't cluster like this.
METHOD 3
Rate Consistency Analysis
Real attackers send bursts. Unnaturally constant inter-packet timing (low coefficient of variation) = automated rate-limiting bot.
